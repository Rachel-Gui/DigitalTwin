# Partner artwork sources

- UW signature and W: https://www.washington.edu/brand/graphic-elements/logo-guidelines/
- SIU white horizontal logo: https://ucomm.siu.edu/brand/logo/
- URBiiLAB original logo: https://www.urbiilab.com/
- Seattle vector mark: inline SVG from https://www.seattle.gov/arts/about-us/download-our-logo
- DRCC white circular logo: https://www.drcc.org/

The partner section uses independent artwork and live HTML text. The IDL name is typeset beside the official UW W; it is not a downloaded IDL logo lockup. The existing SIL artwork is displayed at 64px from a 374px source.

## Official color artwork

- UW purple and gold stacked signatures and block W PNGs: https://www.washington.edu/brand/brand-elements/logos/ . Gold artwork is used on the dark theme for contrast.
- SIU horizontal full-color artwork, black-text and white-text variants: https://ucomm.siu.edu/_common/documents/siu-logo-horizontal-png.zip , linked from https://ucomm.siu.edu/brand/logo/ .
- URBiiLAB uses its original white-and-red artwork from https://www.urbiilab.com/ on a dark backing; the grayscale/invert filter is bypassed.
- SIL, Seattle, and DRCC retain their existing monochrome official/project artwork; no unverified colors were introduced.
