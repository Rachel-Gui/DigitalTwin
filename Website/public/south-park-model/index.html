SCENE DATA & SOURCES

This independent page uses the same published South Park building, road and ground GLB files, without changing their geometry, scale, materials or placement.

The 1,432 building heights are restored from the original Grasshopper Height field. Their original survey date and accuracy have not been independently verified.

The original local GIS model is registered to matching Seattle building FEATURE_IDs using a robust affine fit. This validates source-data alignment, not survey accuracy. Sensor cloud colors show point observations; cloud extent and animated density are illustrative. No physical dispersion simulation is run.
